# Design decisions

The site is structured as six acts: invitation, opportunity, mission, companion, evidence, and next step. Judges and institutional partners need a fast grasp of the challenge, a concrete example, and honest boundaries around the work. A child imagined as the player needs agency, understandable choices, and a companion who makes exploration feel approachable. The site speaks to the former audience while showing the experience intended for the latter.

Large, contextual world scenes preserve the relationship between astronaut, companion and lunar base. They are identified as AI-generated production vision rather than presented as screenshots. Editorial typography, generous spacing and short sections support scanning and chunk complex ideas into manageable steps. The interactive scenario connects a single choice to a system-level consequence without claiming to model real lunar engineering.

Dark overlays support contrast over artwork; text is placed outside busy focal areas. Native links, buttons and disclosure controls support keyboard navigation. Focus indicators, responsive layouts and reduced-motion handling are included. The site uses local assets and system fonts to avoid runtime dependencies. Accessibility should still be checked with real users and assistive technology.
